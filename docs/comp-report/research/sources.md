# Sources — CR-R bibliography

**CR-R · Dispatch 04 · Surveyor · 2026-09-24.** Every source cited in [`adjustments.md`](adjustments.md)
and [`glossary-draft.md`](glossary-draft.md), keyed by the `[key]` used there. All were accessed on
2026-09-24 unless a date says otherwise.

**Each entry gives:** the citation; the publication date and, where it matters, the data period; the
URL; **access** — free, paywalled, or blocked to automated reading; and **read** — what was actually read:
the full text, the abstract only, a page rendered by a web tool, or a secondary report about the source.
`[>5y]` marks a source whose data, or publication where no data period applies, ends before about
September 2021.

**How the research was done.** Nine research passes ran in parallel, one per group of factors plus two
for the glossary; every magnitude they returned carried a verbatim quote from the page read, and Surveyor
re-checked the load-bearing figures against the saved source texts before writing them up. Three limits
apply throughout:
- **StreetEasy** blocks automated reading of its site. Its blog posts cited here were read through a
  public reader service (r.jina.ai), and its help-centre articles through the help centre's public API;
  both are marked.
- **Paywalled academic papers** were read from free working-paper versions where one exists — marked —
  or as abstracts only.
- **A shared search allowance** ran out partway through the research; `adjustments.md` lists what went
  unsearched.
