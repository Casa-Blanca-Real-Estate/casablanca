# Phase 1 test portraits — credits

Kimani's own hard portraits weren't in hand when Phase 1 was validated, so the
comparison sheets here use openly-licensed stand-ins picked for the exact failure
modes the dispatch names: fine/curly hair, flyaway strands, a bright background
that fringes, and a low-contrast edge.

Every sheet is BEFORE (the shipped pipeline) vs AFTER (this branch), rendered
through the tool's own composite path at 1080px on `#c8beb8` and on the dark IG
green `#4e5b41`, with a 400% crop of the edge.
