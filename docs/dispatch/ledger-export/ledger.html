How a track checks off its line

When a track opens its PR, it marks its line delivered from its own session with the ArtifactData tool: update on this artifact, collection dispatches, the line's id (R12·4 on the line is r12-4), data {"status": "delivered", "pr": 1234, "updatedBy": "Thinker"}.

Casanova marks a line landed when it merges the work, and marks any line a track could not reach. Statuses: queued (written) → sent (passed to the track) → delivered (PR open) → landed (merged). Held parks a line; dropped retires it.
