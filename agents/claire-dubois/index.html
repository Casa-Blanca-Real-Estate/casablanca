About Claire

Claire's approach

Claire came to New York from Paris fifteen years ago and never left — which might explain why she has such a particular eye for space and light. She specializes in Tribeca, SoHo, and the West Village, where she's built a reputation for representing both buyers and sellers at the top of the market. She's negotiated dozens of deals over ask, and she's candid when a property isn't worth it.
