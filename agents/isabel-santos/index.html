About Isabel

Isabel's approach

Isabel grew up in Brooklyn and has watched its neighborhoods evolve from the inside. She specializes in Park Slope, Prospect Heights, and Crown Heights — areas she loves for their townhouse stock, community feel, and access to Prospect Park. She's particularly skilled with brownstone transactions and works with a network of inspectors, attorneys, and contractors who make the process seamless from offer to move-in.
