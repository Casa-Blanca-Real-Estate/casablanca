Questions answered

Start your real estate
career the right way.

We hear the same questions from everyone who reaches out. Here are the honest answers.

Apply now
No experience required. Many of our best agents came from hospitality, finance, teaching, and other fields. What matters is drive and a willingness to learn. We provide all the training and mentorship you need.
Our splits are among the most competitive in New York. New agents start at 70/30 and move to 80/20 after their first six deals. We don't charge desk fees or monthly minimums.
The NYS salesperson license requires 77 hours of coursework and a state exam. Most people complete it in 6–8 weeks while working another job. We'll guide you through the whole process.
Transferring your license is straightforward. Reach out and we'll explain the process — it typically takes less than a week. We won't ask you to walk away from active deals you're working.
Leads are assigned based on neighborhood specialty and availability. Buyer and rental leads come through our platform and are pushed directly to your phone. You have 24 hours to respond before they're reassigned.
We accept both full-time and part-time agents. That said, our most successful agents treat this like a business. If you're part-time, we'll set expectations up front so everyone's on the same page.
